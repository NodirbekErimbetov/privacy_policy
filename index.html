World Flags — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Introduction

World Flags does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the World Flags mobile game for Android ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any account details. You do not need to sign up or log in.

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following game data on your device only:

  • Your chosen language
  • Sound and vibration settings
  • Lesson progress: stars, scores, unlocked regions, diamonds and best scores
  • Your daily play streak

This data never leaves your device through the App. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions and internet access

The App requests no special Android permissions. It does not use the internet, so it works fully offline. All flags, maps, pictures and translations are built into the App.

Vibration feedback uses the standard Android haptic feature and needs no permission.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The App contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. There are no in-app purchases. Diamonds and stars are in-game rewards only and have no real-world value.

The landmark photos in the App come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under their open licenses. Their authors and licenses are listed in Settings → Photo credits. Showing these photos does not involve any data exchange.

Android backup. If you have turned on Google's device backup, Android may include the App's saved game data in your personal backup. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, not by us. You can turn it off in your device settings.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for players of all ages, including children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). Children can play without creating an account or sharing any details.

Deleting your data

Your game data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time in either of these ways:

  • In the App: Settings → Reset progress
  • On your device: uninstall the App, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → World Flags

Changes to this policy

If the App ever starts to collect or share data, we will update this policy before that change and update the date at the top. Please check this page from time to time.
